# PixelQuest Screenshots Directory

The images in the main `README.md`, from the new design (UI v2) on main. Each is the app's own screen, rendered from its composables with Robolectric on a 380 × 800 dp phone, with sample data. The Quran list is drawn from the app's bundled Quran files.

- `banner.png`: the banner: Home and Deen in Comic, Quests and Hero in Pixel.
- `v2-home-comic.png`, `v2-home-pixel.png`: Home, with the bottom bar.
- `v2-quests-comic.png`, `v2-quests-pixel.png`: Quests, on its Today tab.
- `v2-deen-comic.png`, `v2-deen-pixel.png`: Deen: the next prayer, Darood Party and the Quran.
- `v2-hero-comic.png`, `v2-hero-pixel.png`: Hero: Profile in Comic, Stats in Pixel.
- `v2-quran.png`: the Quran's surah list, with Continue reading.
- `v2-parties.png`: Parties, with an invitation, a Darood Party and a study party.
- `v2-coach.png`: the AI Coach's brief.
- `v2-settings.png`: Settings, with Look (Comic or Pixel).
