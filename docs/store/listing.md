## 6. Screenshot Specifications & Plan

Google Play requires a minimum of 2 phone screenshots (up to 8) and tablet screenshots for tablet-optimized store listings:

### Formatting Rules
* **Format:** JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha).
* **Dimensions:** Min 320 px, Max 3840 px. Aspect ratio 16:9 or 9:16 (standard phone: 1080 × 2400 px or 1440 × 3200 px).
* **Tablet:** At least 1 screenshot for 7-inch tablet and 1 for 10-inch tablet if claiming tablet support.
