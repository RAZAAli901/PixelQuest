PixelQuest

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 7 November 2026 • Version 1.2.0+

1. Core Principles

  • Local-First by Default: All your daily habit quests, schedule alarms, completion logs, streaks, prayer logs, and Quran bookmarks remain strictly on your device. PixelQuest operates completely without an account.
  • Zero Tracking or Advertising: PixelQuest contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no behavioral tracking, and no data broker integrations.
  • Minimal Cloud Footprint: When you choose to sign in to participate in leaderboards or multiplayer parties, we collect only the minimal data necessary to power those features.
  • Full User Control: You can export your entire data archive or permanently delete your account and public record at any time.

2. Information That Remains on Your Phone

The following data is never sent to our servers. It resides solely in local device storage:

  • Habit Quests: Titles, notes, difficulty, schedule times, snooze settings, and completion history.
  • Spiritual Records: On-device Namaz prayer status, Darood counter tallies, and Quran reader bookmarks.
  • Device Location: If prayer calculations are enabled, approximate location coordinates (city-level) are calculated entirely on-device using local astronomical algorithms and are never logged or transmitted.

3. Cloud Data Collection & Use

If you create a cloud account (via Google Sign-In or email verification code), the following data is processed:

Category Data Elements Purpose
Authentication Email address, unique user UUID Account sign-in, session authorization, and security.
Public Leaderboard Chosen pseudonym, avatar ID, level, XP, streak Public ranking (only visible after explicit opt-in).
Multiplayer & Study Party name, study goals, chat messages, study file attachments, chess moves Facilitating cooperative group accountability and game states.
AI Habit Coach Anonymized completion statistics (e.g. 80% weekly consistency) Generating personalized motivation via Google Gemini proxy. Personal identifiers and quest titles are never transmitted.

4. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

  • Supabase: Cloud database, authentication, and encrypted file storage enforcing strict PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS).
  • Google Gemini API: Generative AI habit coaching for signed-in users. Data is governed under Google Cloud Gemini enterprise privacy terms (prompts are not used to train models).
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Delivers silent push signals for real-time multiplayer chess turns. Payloads carry IDs only; all display strings are constructed locally.

5. Security & Data Rights

Encryption: All network traffic is strictly transmitted over HTTPS/TLS 1.3 with pinned certificate verification. Local authentication credentials on the phone are sealed using hardware-backed Android Keystore cryptography.

Export Data: You may export all your cloud data at any time from Settings → Account → Export cloud data.

Delete Account: You can permanently purge your cloud data in the app under Settings → Account → Delete my cloud data, or online at our Account Deletion Web Portal.

6. Children's Privacy & Contact

PixelQuest is designed for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

For questions or privacy inquiries, contact us at privacy@pixelquest.app.
