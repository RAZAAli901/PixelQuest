Account & Data Deletion

Request permanent deletion of your PixelQuest cloud account and associated data.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can delete your cloud account immediately at any time directly inside the PixelQuest Android app without waiting for manual verification:

  1. Open PixelQuest on your Android device.
  2. Tap Settings (gear icon) in the navigation bar.
  3. Tap Account (or tap your profile in the top-right).
  4. Scroll down to the Danger zone section.
  5. Tap Delete my cloud data.
  6. Confirm both confirmation prompts to permanently purge your cloud profile and public records.
Instant Effect: In-app deletion executes an atomic database procedure that removes your cloud authentication, public leaderboard profile, chat records, and uploaded files immediately.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

If you have uninstalled the app or lost access to your device, submit the form below or email privacy@pixelquest.app using the email registered with your account.

Requests submitted via web form or email are verified and processed within 7 business days.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account

  • Deleted Immediately: Your Supabase user account, registered email address, public display name, avatar choice, leaderboard rankings, and all chat attachments/files you uploaded.
  • Local Data: Local quest titles, checklists, and offline streak records stored in your device's private SQLite database remain on your phone unless you choose to uninstall the app or tap "Reset Progress" in Settings.
  • Anonymized Records: Aggregated party metrics (e.g. historical group Darood count milestones and finished multiplayer chess game boards) are retained in an anonymized state with all author identifiers stripped so remaining members do not experience corrupted game states.
