# PixelQuest v1.3.0 — The Namaz Tracker

Installs over 1.2.0 and keeps everything. The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

### 🕌 Namaz Tracker (optional, off until you turn it on)
- Prayer times worked out on the phone, offline. Five methods are available, with Hanafi or Shafi Asr and per-prayer adjustments.
- Five prayer buttons, Fajr to Isha. Each opens at its time, and you tap it to mark it prayed.
- The screen shows a countdown, the day's progress, the last seven days and an all-five streak.
- Reminders at each prayer, with Prayed, Snooze and Dismiss, and they come back after a restart.
- XP added to the game's own XP:
  - 10 a prayer, 5 if late, and +30 for all five. At most 80 a day.
  - +100 for each 7 days in a row with all five.
  - Undo takes it back.
- Your place and prayer log stay on the phone. The leaderboard sees only your XP total.

### 🧭 Side menu
- The three-line icon at the top left opens your profile, the Namaz Tracker, History, the Leaderboard, Account and Settings.

### 🤖 Compact AI Coach
- The coach card takes less room: a slim header, the latest insight in three lines (expand it for more), and earlier insights as one-line rows.

The release workflow publishes this version's notes from CHANGELOG.md.
