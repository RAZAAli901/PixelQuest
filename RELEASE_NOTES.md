# PixelQuest v1.6.0 — A new look: one app, two skins

Installs over 1.5.0 (or 1.4.1, 1.4.0, 1.3.0) and keeps everything. The full list is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

### 🎨 A new design
- A bottom bar in place of the side menu: Home, Quests, the trophy, Deen and Hero.
- Two skins, Comic and Pixel, in Settings > Look. If you'd chosen Clean Light or Follow System, it opens in Comic.
- Every screen rebuilt: Home, Quests, Deen, Hero, the Quran, parties, the AI Coach, Settings and every dialog.
- One red button per screen, and icons in place of emoji.
- A new app icon. Notifications have the new icon and colours.

### ⚡ Smoother and quicker
- No refresh circle when you open a party: it checks the server quietly, and the circle shows only when you pull.
- Tab switches don't reload, a quicker start, and the app is compiled ahead of time when it's installed.
- Smaller: 4.6 MB, down from 7.4.

### 🛠️ Improved
- Notifications are asked for the first time you turn a reminder on, not at launch.

The release workflow publishes this version's notes from CHANGELOG.md.
