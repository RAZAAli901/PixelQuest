MOTION SYSTEM
Every win should feel physical.
Twelve small animations for the moments that matter: XP landing in the bar, a quest ticking off, a streak going up, a perfect day. Comic motion is springy and overshoots. Pixel motion moves in hard steps, like an old console.
Each demo loops. The left stage is Comic and the right is Pixel. The Compose versions are in PqMotion.kt.
