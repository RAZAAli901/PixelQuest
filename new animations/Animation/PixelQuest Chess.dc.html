NEW FEATURE
Chess, inside the party.
Challenge a friend or a party member to a game of chess at 1, 3, 5 or 10 minutes. Each pair keeps a running head-to-head score of wins, draws and losses. Winning earns Int, PixelQuest's chess rating, and party members can watch any live game.
The phone screens use the app's own Comic and Pixel looks, so they drop straight into the existing UI. This document is the spec around them.

THE MATHS
Int is an Elo rating. Expected score E = 1 / (1 + 10^((opp − you) / 400)), and after each game Int += K × (result − E), where a win counts 1, a draw ½ and a loss 0. K is 40 for your first 20 games in a pool, then 24. Everyone starts at 800 in each pool and can't drop below 100. Results round to whole points, and both players' changes are worked out on the server.
Example from the mocks: you (1,046) beat @sara (1,102). E = 0.42, so you gain 24 × 0.58 ≈ +14 and she loses 14.
