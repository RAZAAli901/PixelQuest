NOTIFICATION SYSTEM
Meet Pix. Pix reacts.
Pix is the sword badge with a face. Every notification carries Pix in a mood that matches the moment: proud after a perfect day, sweating when a streak is at risk, in tears when it breaks. The launcher icon can switch moods too.
LIVE · CYCLING MOODS
When Pix changes mood he pops, then settles into that mood's idle loop. Use the same pop in the app whenever his state changes.
